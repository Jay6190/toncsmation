About Toncsmation Engineering

Grounded in Tanzania's grid, built to industrial standard

Toncsmation Engineering Solutions was founded on a simple premise: industrial and commercial clients in East Africa deserve electrical work that meets the same standard as anywhere in the world, delivered by engineers who understand local terrain, permitting, and grid conditions.

From our Dar es Salaam base, our licensed engineers and technicians plan, install, and maintain the electrical backbone of factories, commercial sites, and public infrastructure — backed by an in-house materials supply chain that keeps projects moving without costly import delays.
